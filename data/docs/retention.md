# Data retention
This is a fictional AcmeDesk handbook used only for the demo.

## Deleted records
Deleted records remain in the recovery bin for 30 days before permanent deletion.
A workspace administrator can restore records during that recovery period.
Records permanently deleted from the recovery bin cannot be restored through the application.

## Backups
Service backups are retained for 7 days for disaster recovery.
Backups are not a user-facing archive and cannot be searched from the dashboard.
