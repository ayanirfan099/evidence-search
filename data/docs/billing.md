# Billing
This is a fictional AcmeDesk handbook used only for the demo.

## Invoices
Workspace owners can download invoices from Settings, Billing, Invoice history.
Invoices are generated on the first day of each monthly billing cycle.
Update the billing address before the next invoice is generated.

## Refunds
Refund requests must be submitted within 14 days of the initial purchase.
Approved refunds normally reach the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.
Renewal refunds are reviewed individually by the billing team.
