# Workspace onboarding
This is a fictional AcmeDesk handbook used only for the demo.

## First steps
Create a workspace, verify the owner email and invite a second administrator.
Import the sample CSV to explore dashboards before importing your own records.
The sample workspace contains fictional data and can be reset from Settings, Demo data.

## Training
The introductory webinar lasts 45 minutes and includes a live question session.
Training recordings are available to workspace members on the learning page.
